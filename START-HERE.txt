GET YOUR .JAR WITH NO INSTALLS (about 5 minutes)

1. Make a free account at github.com and click New repository (any name). Create it.
2. On the new repo page click "uploading an existing file".
3. Unzip this zip, open the folder, select EVERYTHING inside (including the hidden
   ".github" folder) and drag it into the browser. Click Commit changes.
   (Mac: press Cmd+Shift+. in Finder to show the hidden .github folder.)
4. Click the "Actions" tab. Wait ~2 minutes for the green check.
5. Click the finished run, scroll to "Artifacts", download DonutMarket-jar.
6. Unzip that and put donutmarket-1.0.0.jar in your server's plugins folder. Restart.

Server must be Paper 26.2 on Java 25.
